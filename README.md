`main.py` writes one funny passive-aggressive note, 103 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

**Your diet starts Monday, says the note on my door. It is Thursday. I have stopped believing in Mondays.**

The owner taped a diet promise to the fridge door; the fridge quotes it
back, checks the calendar, and gives up on Mondays altogether. Dry,
petty, specific — and it stays funny the second time, because every
fridge in the world has watched a Monday diet die by Thursday.
